## Decisions

- **A clean pass still lands its pull request.** `curate.md` §0.2 is explicit:
  the cycle's date is the base-branch commit that DELETES a
  `docs/handover/curate-*.md`, so a pass that lands nothing clears nothing and
  the next session is handed the identical item for ever. The role carries the
  measurement that bought the rule — this repository read `curate : DUE` on
  every pass, and under unsupervised the heartbeat re-seeds sessions that each
  curate, land nothing and re-arm the trigger.
- **Section 2 runs; 3 and 4 have nothing in them.** §0.2 would have skipped
  all three, and the hand audit (r1) is why it does not: one declaration is
  in a form the shape rule forbids. DECLUTTER has no signal to check against
  merged history, and PROPOSE has no decompose candidate (max 4 Scope
  bullets, threshold 8) and no exclusive collision — the one doubly-declared
  path, `joharness.sh`, is marked `shared:` on BOTH plans, which is what
  voids a wave split rather than one side's marking.
- **The audit is the pass, not the reader's verdict.** A curator that accepts
  a clean line has done nothing, and the whole output would have rested on
  one line of tool text. The six checks and what each returned are in r1 and
  r2.
- **The 2026-09-17 stamp stays.** The pass was claimed and pushed that day
  and the branch has held it since; the role keys the CYCLE off "the newest
  base-branch commit deleting a `docs/handover/curate-*.md`", which is the
  retire commit's date and will read 2026-10-05. Renaming the file would
  change the identity `dispatch` has been reading in flight for three weeks.
- **This is `NOTHING TO CURATE`, not `NOTHING READ`**, and the pull request
  body says which. The second means the queue is empty or every plan is
  HELD — a pass with no subject rather than a clean one. Here 5 plans were
  read and every declaration held. A reader told only "nothing" cannot tell
  those apart, which is why the role separates them.

## Rejected

- **Widening the pass to find something to do.** The queue is 5 plans and 3
  open questions and a curator could always find prose to improve. The role's
  `## Never` list is the boundary: no splitting, no reordering, no `urgency:`,
  no requirement, no research file, no protocol path — and no plan body beyond
  a stale anchor. What the audit did NOT do is go looking for prose to
  improve: the one repair it made is a declaration in a form the shape rule
  forbids, which is a declaration fact and squarely §2's. A clean verdict is
  a result rather than a failure to look hard enough — but it is the
  READER's result, and checking it is not widening the pass.
- **Skipping the pull request because the diff is empty.** That is the exact
  failure §0.2 names, and this repository is the one it was measured in.
