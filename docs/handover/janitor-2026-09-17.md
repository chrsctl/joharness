  command's own output says liveness is not in it. Every release below cites
  the reading that proved it, per the field table in

  FAILED bucket confirmed by a second read with `updated_at` and the head
  both unchanged. `IDLE` alone is between turns, not gone, whatever the push
  age says.

  safe direction is one sweep's delay; the cost of the other is destroyed
  work in progress and a duplicate session on a live branch.
